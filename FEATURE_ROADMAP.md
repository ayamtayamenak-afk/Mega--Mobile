Speedhack; Instant Complete; Auto Clicker; Jump Hack; Freeze Player;
No Hitbox; Hitbox Multiplier; No Solids; No Spikes; Everything Hurt;
Force Block Type; Auto Kill; Auto Pickup Coins; 0% Practice Complete;
Show Layout; Show Trajectory; Frame Stepper; P1/P2 Input Mirror/Switch;
Reverse Inputs.

## 7. AI Clipsy
Rekomendasi level, bantuan berbasis teks, Rainbow Wave Trail,
Rainbow Robot Fire, Rainbow Trail, Accurate Hitbox, dan Auto Play Level.

AI Clipsy membutuhkan desain terpisah. Rekomendasi berbasis AI online
memerlukan layanan/model dan koneksi; tidak disediakan oleh kerangka ini.
Auto Play juga memerlukan engine input dan validasi khusus.
